Three ways to say
the same thing.

አንድ ነገር በሦስት መንገድ

One profile, built three times in three different design canons. Same facts, different argument. Pick the one that sounds like him — or keep all three and send a different link to a different room.

Canon 01 · Heritage Noir Recommended

Obsidian & Ge'ez

ጥቁር ወርቅ

Obsidian ground, velvet teal, liquid amber. Cinzel display over DM Sans, with Amharic treated as a first-class voice rather than a subtitle. The ጄ sigil is the mark. Built for a Pan-Africanist Ethiopian technologist — the only one of the three where the Amharic carries equal weight to the English.

Canon 02 · Editorial Minimal

Paper & Ink

ጋዜጣዊ ገጽታ

Warm paper, Fraunces over Inter, drop cap, asymmetric twelve-column grid. The type does the work and the portrait arrives after the argument, never before it. Reads like a press profile rather than a landing page — the safest of the three if this link is going anywhere professional.

Canon 03 · Scroll Cinema

The Camera

የሚንቀሳቀስ ገጽ

Five scenes, one rAF loop, an eased camera. Addis at dusk, carved Ge'ez relief, blockchain topology, then the record. Text fades on scene position, so nothing moves while you are reading it. The showpiece — heavy, but nobody forgets it.
